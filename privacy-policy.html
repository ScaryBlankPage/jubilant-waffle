Special Note on Epic Account Data (The "Permitted Uses" Rule)

In accordance with the EOS Developer Agreement, all Epic Account Data (unique account identifier, display name, friends lists, online status) is considered **Epic-Owned Data**.

We adhere to the strict rule that we will only use this data for the Permitted Uses:

  1. Authenticating users.
  2. Providing Epic’s social features in connection with your Games.
  3. Assisting users in making social connections across other services (ONLY with your explicit consent).

We will not sell, rent, license, disclose, or transfer Epic Account Data to any third party for monetary or other valuable consideration.

3. Data Storage, Security, and Cross-Border Transfers

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the personal data we control from unauthorized or accidental processing, access, destruction, loss, or disclosure. These measures include secure software development safeguards and access control policies.

Data Transfers (International)

As an EOS Developer, your data may be processed outside of the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States, by Epic Games and its service providers.

  • In the event we access Epic Account Data from a location outside of an adequacy-determined jurisdiction (such as the UK or EEA), the processing is governed by the **Controller-to-Controller Standard Contractual Clauses** as incorporated by the EOS Developer Agreement.
  • By using our Games, you acknowledge and agree to the transfer of your data to these locations for the purposes outlined in this policy.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the UK/EU GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@frostydoggames.com.

Your Right Description
The Right to Access To request copies of your personal data held by us.
The Right to Rectification To request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") To request that we delete your personal data.
The Right to Restrict Processing To request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
The Right to Object to Processing To object to the processing of your personal data (e.g., for direct marketing).
The Right to Data Portability To request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Mechanism (Action Point)

We will only retain your Epic Account Data for as long as necessary for the Permitted Uses or as required by applicable law.

User Deletion Requests

In accordance with the EOS Addendum, we commit to securely deleting your personal data in our possession upon request.

To request the deletion of your personal data from Frosty Dog Games and the Epic Online Services backend, please send an email to privacy@frostydoggames.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request."

We will promptly and securely delete all of your Epic Account Data and all related in-game data (stats, achievements, save files) upon receiving your request or upon notification from Epic that you have submitted a deletion request to them.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Games are aimed at the casual gamer market. **They are not primarily directed to children under the age of 13.**

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under the age of 13 in a manner not permitted by law, we will immediately cease using the Service for that user and delete the data.

7. Third-Party Service Providers and Sharing

We only share data with third parties necessary to provide the service. These include:

  • **Epic Games, Inc. and its Affiliates:** For account authentication, social features, game services (stats, achievements), and telemetry. Epic acts as the Data Controller for Epic Account Data.
  • **Analytics Providers:** For analyzing game performance and user behavior (excluding Epic Account Data).
  • **Cloud Hosting Providers:** For hosting our website and backend data storage.
